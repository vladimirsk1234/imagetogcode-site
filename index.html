Photo → CNC G-code

From a picture to a toolpath.

iMage-to-Gcode is for machinists and hobbyists who want a simple path from a photo to a file the machine can run. Take or choose a picture, prepare it, generate G-code, preview the cut, then share the file.

On your device

How it works

A short shop-floor loop. Nothing here requires an account.

01 · Photo

Take or choose a picture

Use the camera or pick an image from your library — a sketch, a logo, or a part photo.

02 · Prepare

Clean up what will be cut

Crop, adjust contrast, invert, and set the workpiece size so the picture matches the job.

03 · Mode

Sculpting or Contour

Sculpting fills the image as a carved surface. Contour follows the outlines.

04 · Generate

Make the G-code

The app builds a CNC toolpath from the prepared picture, on the phone.

05 · Preview

Watch the path first

Play a simulation of the cut so you can review the motion before you share the file.

06 · Share

Send the file

Share the G-code through the system share sheet — Files, Drive, AirDrop, or mail.

Two ways to cut

Pick the style that matches the job. You can switch and generate again.

Sculpting

Carve the picture

Use Sculpting when you want the image itself — shaded areas become the machined surface, like a relief or engraved plate.

Contour

Follow the outlines

Use Contour when the edges matter — the toolpath traces the shapes in the photo instead of filling them.

Also in the app

Recents

Finished jobs stay on the phone so you can open them again, replay the preview, or share the file. Delete a recent whenever you want.

Share when you choose

Nothing is sent anywhere unless you tap Share and pick a destination. The file leaves the app only through that step.

Photos, settings, and recent jobs stay on your device. See the Privacy Policy for details, or contact support if you need help.
