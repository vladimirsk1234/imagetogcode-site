What to include

A short note is enough. These details help:

  • Device and system (for example iPhone + iOS, or Android)
  • What you were doing (photo, prepare, generate, preview, share, Recents)
  • Sculpting or Contour, if it matters
  • What you expected vs what happened

You do not need to send the photo or G-code unless you are comfortable sharing it.

Safety

Always review generated G-code before machining. You own feeds, speeds, work holding, and path proving. Preview is a guide — not a substitute for shop practice.
