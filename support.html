Support

iMage-to-Gcode · Vladimir Skliar

If something in the app is unclear, a generate failed, or you have a question before you run a file on a machine — write to us.

Email

imagetogcode@gmail.com

This is the primary support address for App Store and Google Play listings. We aim to reply within a few business days. Response times can be longer around weekends and holidays.

What to include

A short note is enough. These details help us answer faster:

  • Device and system version (for example iPhone 15, iOS 18 — or the Android equivalent)
  • What you were doing (take photo, prepare, generate, preview, share, Recents)
  • Sculpting or Contour, if it matters to the issue
  • What you expected, and what happened instead

You do not need to send the photo or G-code unless you are comfortable doing so. If you attach a file, treat it as something you chose to share with us for that request.

Safety reminder

Always review a generated file before you run it on a machine. You are responsible for feeds, speeds, work holding, and proving the path. The preview in the app is a guide, not a substitute for shop practice.
