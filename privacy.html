Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 8, 2026 · Operator: Vladimir Skliar / iMage-to-Gcode

This policy explains how the iMage-to-Gcode mobile app (“the app”) handles information. It is written in plain language for Apple App Store and Google Play review, and for anyone who uses the app.

Who we are

iMage-to-Gcode is operated by Vladimir Skliar. For privacy questions, email iMagetoGcode@Gmail.com.

The short version

  • Photos and projects stay on your device.
  • The app prepares images and builds G-code on the device.
  • You do not need an account for core use.
  • A file leaves the app only if you use Share and pick a destination.
  • The current app does not include analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting products in its listed dependencies.

What the app does

You take or choose a photo, prepare it, generate a CNC toolpath (G-code), preview the path, and optionally share the file. Recent jobs can be saved on the phone so you can open them again.

Information on your device

The app may store the following locally on your phone or tablet:

  • Photos you take or choose, while you work on them
  • Edits you make (for example crop, contrast, invert)
  • Machine and size settings you enter
  • Generated G-code files
  • A list of recent jobs, until you delete them

This information is kept in the app’s on-device storage. We do not run a product server that receives your photos, settings, or G-code as part of normal use.

Permissions

The app asks for access only when a feature needs it:

  • Camera — to take a photo, or to preview workpiece size on a real surface.
  • Photo library — to choose an existing picture.

You can refuse a permission in the system dialog. You can later change access in your device settings. If camera access is off, you can still choose a photo from the library (where the system allows it).

Sharing

If you tap Share, the system share sheet opens. You choose where the G-code file goes (for example Files, a drive app, AirDrop, or email). Those destinations are not part of iMage-to-Gcode. Their own policies apply once you send the file.

The app does not upload your file in the background.

Accounts

Core use does not require creating an account, signing in, or providing your name or email inside the app.

Analytics, advertising, and crash reports

We reviewed the current mobile app’s listed dependencies. We did not find analytics, advertising, attribution, or crash-reporting software (for example products commonly used to count users, show ads, or upload crash logs).

The app does not ask you to accept tracking for advertising. If we add measurement, ads, or crash reporting later, we will update this policy before that change reaches a store build.

Apple, Google, and your device maker may collect their own diagnostics when you download or use apps. That collection is governed by their policies, not by us.

Children

The app is a shop tool for adults. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us by email, contact us and we will delete it.

How long information stays, and how you delete it

You control what remains on the device:

  • Delete a recent job in the app to remove that saved project and its stored file.
  • Remove the working photo by starting a new photo or leaving the job.
  • Uninstall the app to remove the app’s local data, subject to how your operating system works.

If you email us for support, we keep that correspondence only as long as needed to answer you and keep a minimal record of the request, then delete it when it is no longer needed.

Email you send us

If you write to iMagetoGcode@Gmail.com, we receive whatever you include (your address, the message, and any attachments). We use that only to respond and to improve the product when you describe a problem. We do not sell this information.

App stores and this website

Downloads through the Apple App Store or Google Play are handled by those stores. This marketing and legal site is hosted as GitHub Pages. GitHub may process standard web logs when you visit these pages. We do not embed advertising or analytics scripts on this site.

Changes

If the app’s data practices change, we will update this page and the effective date. For a store listing, the URL of this policy will remain the privacy link.
